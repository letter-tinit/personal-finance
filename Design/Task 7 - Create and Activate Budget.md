# Task 7 - Create and Activate a Budget

## Draft

```text
Salary Budget

Monthly salary
[16,000,000                         ]

Budget method
[50/30/20] [6 Jars]

Budget preview
Needs                         8,000,000
Wants                         4,800,000
Savings                       3,200,000

[Create Budget]
```

- Salary and method are editable.
- The bucket list is a preview and updates immediately.
- `Create Budget` is disabled when salary is empty, invalid, or not greater than zero.

## Active

```text
Salary Budget

Monthly salary                 16,000,000
Budget method                    50/30/20

Budget buckets
Needs                         8,000,000
Wants                         4,800,000
Savings                       3,200,000
```

- Salary and method are displayed as read-only values.
- The method picker and `Create Budget` button are no longer shown.
- Transaction creation is intentionally absent until Task 8.
